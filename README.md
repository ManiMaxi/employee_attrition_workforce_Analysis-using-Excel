#### **Employee Attrition \& Workforce Analytics**



###### **Project Overview** 

&#x09;This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce data using Microsoft Excel. The project includes data cleaning, data validation, KPI analysis, business analysis, and an interactive dashboard.



###### **Tools Used**

* Microsoft Excel
* PivotTables
* PivotCharts
* Slicers
* Excel Formulas



###### **Analysis**

* The project analyzes:
* Employee attrition
* Department
* Job role
* Tenure
* Salary
* Overtime



###### **Key Findings**

* Overall Attrition rate is 14.91%
* Operations has the highest attrition rate among departments at 15.56%
* Finance has the lowest attrition rate among departments at 13.74%
* Supply Chain Analyst has the highest attrition rate among job roles at 19.04%
* HR Analyst has the lowest attrition rate among job roles at 12.56%
* Employees who work overtime has the highest attrition rate (21.74%) than employees who don’t(12.28%)
* The 40-60k salary group has the highest attrition rate  among salary groups at 16%
* The 80-110k salary group has the lowest attrition rate among salary groups at 13.26%
* The highest attrition rate is  seen the above one year at 18.27%
* The lowest attrition rate is seen the 3 to 5 years at 13.79%
